04 · Career plan — understanding the job

Goal: IT planning, business analysis and system design. Games, education and data are domains; PM is a role that comes after years of experience. My first job will be as a developer — as I heard in mentoring, "Companies don't hire PMs straight out of school." I'll do planning once I have the experience to lead a team. Until then, I'll learn to analyze people's needs and design systems while I develop. I broke the job into four parts.

What the job is

Analyzing what people need and turning it into requirements → specifications → design (UML · ERD · BPMN). Games are just one example domain — designing game rules and numbers is the same kind of work.

What it takes

The basics: knowing how to handle data. Build things myself, and be able to explain why AI built something the way it did. Keep data in one place, without duplicates, and up to date.

How I'm preparing

Four goals for 2027: one finished piece each quarter, at a link anyone can open · build things myself and draw one full set of designs · ADsP (Advanced Data Analytics Semi-Professional) · turn my notes into my own knowledge. I'll also practice a two-page proposal summary for one public-sector RFP.

Looking at the job market

Companies hire for a role. A résumé only needs to reach the one place that will be mine, so my goal has to fit in one line to aim well. The "projects won and work done" of my three target companies are not sorted out yet.

How to explain my-setup-proj

A writing template

  1. The problem — "My experiences — directing for the broadcasting club, my web portfolio, running content channels — sat apart and never added up to one story. Having no structure to join them was the limit I felt."
  2. The aim — "I started my-setup-proj to gather scattered knowledge and experience into one project structure and raise the value of that knowledge." (The point isn't a list of experiences, but the skills that only show once they're joined.)
  3. The trigger — "After advice on system design in Codyssey AI mentoring, I decided to design a system myself, using my love of analyzing and studying."
  4. The structure — List what I've made so far (web portfolio, content work, concepts I've learned), then show in a diagram or outline which module or role each one plays inside my-setup-proj.
  5. What's next — "I'll keep growing it into my own store of data and know-how, the base of a planning, analysis and design portfolio."
